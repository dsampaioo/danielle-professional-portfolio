# Customer Service Sample 01

## Professional Customer Support

Project Type: Customer Communication

Example 1 – Customer Inquiry

Customer:
Hello! I would like to know more about your product. Is it available in different sizes?

My Response:
Hello! Thank you for your interest. Yes, our product is available in different sizes. I would be happy to help you find the best option for your needs. Please let me know if you have any other questions!

Example 2 – Order Delay

Customer:
Hi! My order has not arrived yet. Can you help me?

My Response:
Hello! I'm sorry for the delay and understand your concern. I'd be happy to help you check your order status. Could you please provide your order number so I can assist you?

Example 3 – Customer Complaint

Customer:
I received my order, but the product is different from what I expected.

My Response:
Hello! I'm sorry to hear that your experience did not meet your expectations. Your satisfaction is important to us. Could you please share more details about the issue so we can find the best solution?

Skills Demonstrated

- Customer communication
- Professional email and message writing
- Problem-solving
- Customer support
- Polite and friendly communication
- Attention to detail

Note: This is a sample project created to demonstrate customer service skills.
